ABOUT OUR TRUST

Creating Hope Through Education & Care

Parents Rural Educational Development Trust is dedicated to supporting rural communities and improving lives through meaningful social service.

We focus on educational support, food assistance, care for elderly people, and helping families facing difficult circumstances. Through community activities and the support of our well-wishers, we work towards building a more caring and inclusive society.
