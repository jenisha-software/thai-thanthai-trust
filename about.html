OUR STORY

Our History & Mission

Building a caring community through education, support and service.

Parents Rural Educational Development Trust works towards supporting rural communities through education, food assistance, elderly care and community welfare activities.

Our mission is to encourage educational opportunities, support people facing hardship, and promote a caring community where people can access essential assistance with dignity and respect.

♥

Our Mission

To serve people with compassion and contribute to a stronger, more supportive community.
